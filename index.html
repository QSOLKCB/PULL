100 points per clay. +40 for each extra clay broken with one shot. +250 for clearing a round. Each reload costs 35; each escaped clay costs 15. Round scores bottom out at zero.

Two trigger presses, two barrels. Empty chambers reload automatically in 0.94 seconds. R tops up early and costs the same. Each round starts with two free shells. Your firing position is fixed.

Desktop mouse or standard gamepad required; no touch controls. Controller menus: D-pad to navigate, A to select, B to close. Audio starts after a click. M mutes. Losing focus pauses play.
